The 3 Golden Rules of Fantasy Trading

Maximize value without hurting your starter lineup structure.

  • 1. Upgrade Starters: Trading two bench players for one elite starter is always a win in shallower leagues.
  • 2. Target Bye-Week Panic: Trade for top players when rival managers need immediate wins during bye weeks.
  • 3. Sell High on Touchdown Flukes: Trade away players relying solely on unsustainable touchdown luck.
